The beginning

It started with a passion for food.

Not a boardroom idea. Not a trend. Just the simple belief that good food has a way of bringing people together — and that everyone deserves a shortcut to something delicious.

Havenia Foods is being built one thoughtful product at a time.

Our story

From a love of cooking to a table full of possibilities.

Havenia Foods is the home for food brands made with curiosity, care and a healthy respect for flavour. Our first brand, Vyanjans, brings much-loved Indian flavours into convenient ready-to-cook formats.

And this is only the beginning. As new ideas take shape, more brands and more categories will find their place under Havenia.

“Food should save you time — never the pleasure of eating it.”
